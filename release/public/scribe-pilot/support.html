Scribe Pilot Support

Scribe Pilot is available to invited agency users. Ask your agency administrator for an invitation and access to the approved service.

Getting started

  1. Install Scribe Pilot on your iPhone and paired Apple Watch.
  2. In Settings choose Accept invitation, enter the invitation code and choose your password.
  3. Review the privacy policy and processing disclosure before sending information for AI processing.
  4. Record on either device, select the recording and choose Process. Review and edit the generated draft.

Help with access or recordings

Contact your agency administrator for invitation, membership or password help. Contact Sky Data Services using the link below for technical support. Include the app version, device type, approximate time and a short description. Do not send patient information, recordings, transcripts, passwords or API keys.

Connection interruptions

Keep queued recordings until processing is complete. Reconnect to the service and retry the affected item. An offline Watch may need time to reconnect with the phone before its saved audio transfers.

Account deletion

Open Settings, Privacy and account, then Delete account. Follow the confirmation steps. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your account. See the account-deletion information page for details.

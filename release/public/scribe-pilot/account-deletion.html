Account deletion

In Scribe Pilot open Settings, Privacy and account, then Delete account. Confirm your password and the deletion action. This deletes your active account, recordings, transcripts, results, assistant files and saved conversations, and revokes phone and Watch sessions. Minimal pseudonymous security audit records and deletion tombstones remain. Previously exported copies and historical backups are not recalled by this action.

If you own the service's platform-operator account, initiate the request in the app and contact support to transfer infrastructure ownership first. If you cannot sign in, use the support contact below. Do not include patient information.

Scribe Pilot Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026. Scribe Pilot is an invitation-only recording, transcription, and documentation service operated by Sky Data Services for authorized agency users.

Information processed

The service processes your account and agency membership, voice recordings, transcripts, assistant instructions and reference files you select, generated drafts, conversations, and limited security and operational records. Recordings may contain patient or other sensitive information. Submit only information you are authorized to use.

Purpose and access

Information is used to provide the features you request, administer your agency workspace, protect accounts, and respond to support requests. Agency administrators can review records within their agency. Authorized service operators can access infrastructure for support and security; access is restricted. Routine application logs do not include recording or transcript bodies.

OpenAI processing

When you request AI processing, relevant audio, transcripts, selected reference material, and conversation context are sent to OpenAI through the approved API organization and project. The app asks you to accept the processing disclosure. Responses requests disable application storage. OpenAI's approved Modified Retention setting may still retain encrypted abuse-monitoring data. This service does not promise zero data retention. API input/output sharing for model training is disabled in the configured organization.

Storage and retention

Saved audio, transcripts, results and reference files are encrypted on the service and protected on supported devices. They remain available until deleted through the service. Deleted content is removed from active storage; minimal identifier-only tombstones and security audit events remain to prevent resurrection and document access. Historical backups and previously exported copies are subject to their separate retention and access controls. Deleting an app alone does not delete the service account or server records.

Your controls

You can remove recordings and reference files, withdraw AI-processing permission, sign out, and initiate account deletion in Settings. Withdrawing permission stops new AI requests, but cannot recall information already submitted. Account deletion requires password confirmation. A platform operator must transfer infrastructure ownership before deletion can be completed.

Sharing and other services

We do not sell this information or use it for targeted advertising. Choosing to export, copy or email content sends it to destinations you select; your agency must authorize those destinations. The app does not automatically send recordings to Notion, Gemini or a server mail service. Do not include patient information in support messages.

Clinical use

AI results are drafts and may be inaccurate or incomplete. An authorized clinician must verify the source and correct the draft before using it in documentation or care. Scribe Pilot does not independently diagnose, recommend treatment or replace clinical judgment.

Changes and contact

Material changes to AI processing require a new disclosure version. Use the contact link below for privacy, access, deletion or security questions. This policy does not replace your agency's privacy obligations or its agreements with Sky Data Services.
